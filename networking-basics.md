#  Networking Basics

Today I started learning the fundamentals of networking as part of my cybersecurity journey.

##  Topics I Learned

### 1. What is Networking?

Networking is the process of connecting two or more devices so they can communicate and share data or resources.

### 2. What is a Network?

A network is a group of connected devices that can communicate with each other and share resources.

### 3. The Internet

The Internet is a global network of interconnected networks.

##  Types of Networks

### PAN — Personal Area Network

A PAN connects devices around one person over a short distance.

Example:
- Phone
- Bluetooth headphones

### LAN — Local Area Network

A LAN connects devices within a small/local area.

Examples:
- Home
- School
- Office
- Computer laboratory

### MAN — Metropolitan Area Network

A MAN connects networks across a city or metropolitan area.

### WAN — Wide Area Network

A WAN connects networks across a large geographical area, such as different cities or countries.

## 🔌 Network Devices

### Router

A router connects different networks and directs data between them.

### Switch

A switch connects multiple devices within the same network.

### Modem

A modem helps connect a local network to an Internet Service Provider (ISP).

### Access Point (AP)

An access point allows wireless devices to connect to a network using Wi-Fi.

##  Important Things I Learned

- Networking is the process of connecting devices.
- A network is a group of connected devices.
- PAN is for a personal area.
- LAN is for a local area.
- MAN covers a metropolitan/city area.
- WAN covers a wide geographical area.
- A switch connects devices within a network.
- A router connects different networks.
- An access point provides wireless access to a network.
- A modem helps provide a connection to an ISP.
